# Follow-up templates

Sources: Northeastern University career services on thank-you notes https://careers.northeastern.edu/resources/thank-you-notes-2/ ; CSUN on thank-you timing https://nazariancareers.csun.edu/blog/2025/05/26/when-to-send-a-thank-you-email-after-an-interview/ ; Tufts on following up after applying https://careers.tufts.edu/blog/2022/05/18/answers-from-career-advisors-applied-and-havent-heard-back-here-are-some-next-steps/ ; Robert Half on following up https://www.roberthalf.com/blog/job-interview-tips/should-you-follow-up-after-applying-for-a-job

Keep each one short, specific and in his voice. He sends them.

## Thank-you (within 24 hours of an interview)
Subject: Thank you – [Job title] interview on [Day]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for talking with me today about the [Job title] role. I enjoyed hearing about [one specific thing from the conversation].

[One sentence linking a strength to something they need.]

Thank you again,
[First name]

## Check-in after applying (about a week later, only with a real contact)
Subject: Following up – [Job title] application

Hi [Name],

I applied for the [Job title] role on [date] and wanted to say I'm still very interested. [One line on a relevant strength.] Is there anything else I can send to help?

Thank you,
[First name]

## Still interested (after the decision date passes)
Subject: Checking in – [Job title]

Hi [Name],

I wanted to check in on the [Job title] role. I'm still very interested and happy to answer any questions. Do you have an update on timing?

Thank you,
[First name]

## Thank you for the "no" (keeps the door open)
Subject: Thank you – [Job title]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for letting me know, and for your time. I'd be glad to be considered for future openings on the team.

Best,
[First name]
