# ATS-safe resume rules

Sources: MIT Career Advising & Professional Development, "Make your resume ATS friendly" (modified Apr 23, 2026) https://capd.mit.edu/resources/make-your-resume-ats-friendly/ ; Harvard Office of Career Services, "Create a strong resume" https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume ; CareerOneStop https://blog.careeronestop.org/?p=3772 ; Robert Half, IT help desk resume https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/insights/landing-job/how-to-write-it-help-desk-resume ; Greenhouse application rules https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/203105595-Application-Rules-Overview

## What hiring software really does
- Most systems store and search resumes; recruiters then read them, often in arrival order or filtered by keywords, referrals and screening questions.
- Automatic rejections usually come from **knockout questions** on the application (work authorization, location, schedule, required certificate), not from the resume layout. Answer those carefully and truthfully.
- Columns, tables and text boxes can scramble the reading order, which makes a good resume look messy to the recruiter.

## Layout
- One column. No tables, text boxes, columns, graphics, icons, charts, skill bars or photos.
- Standard headings: Summary, Experience, Skills, Education, Certifications.
- Font: Calibri or Arial, 10.5 to 12 pt body; name 14 to 16 pt. Margins about 1 inch (0.7 minimum).
- Bold for job titles and headings; no colors needed.
- Dates in one style: "Mar 2023 – Present".
- Contact info in the body, not in the page header or footer.

## Files
- Master file: .docx. Send PDF only when a posting asks for it or allows either. Keep a plain-text copy for pasting into forms.
- His own master copy: `Resume - Master.docx`. Files he uploads to employers carry his name, because recruiters see the file name: `Firstname Lastname - Resume - Company.docx`.
- Set the file's author property to his name (some tools default to the tool's name).
- Bullets: use Word's standard round bullet list, not typed symbols or images. In the plain-text copy, start each bullet with "- ".
- Test: save or view as plain text; if anything is missing or jumbled, fix the layout.

## Words
- Mirror the posting's important words for skills he really has (about 5 to 10 terms), and spell out abbreviations once: "Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)".
- Avoid "various", "etc.", "responsible for", "duties included".
- No keyword stuffing, no white or hidden text.

## Length
- Entry level: one page. Cover about the last 10 years.
- Chronological order (newest first). Avoid "functional" resumes that hide dates; recruiters distrust them.
