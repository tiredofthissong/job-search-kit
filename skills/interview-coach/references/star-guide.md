# STAR stories

Source: MIT CAPD, "The STAR method for behavioral interviews" https://capd.mit.edu/resources/the-star-method-for-behavioral-interviews

- **Situation** (about 20%): where and when, one or two sentences.
- **Task** (about 10%): what he needed to do.
- **Action** (about 60%): what **he** did, step by step. Say "I", not "we".
- **Result** (about 10%): what happened, with a number or a quote if possible, and what he learned.

Keep each story to about one minute spoken. Prepare 3 to 5 stories that can answer many questions:
1. An upset customer he turned around.
2. Learning a new system or tool fast.
3. Fixing a problem or figuring something out.
4. Helping or training a coworker.
5. A mistake he owned and fixed.

Practice them as bullet points, not memorized speeches, and keep every detail true.
