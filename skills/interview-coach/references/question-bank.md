# Question bank

Common questions for customer service, chat/email support, and help desk roles. Pick ones that fit the job.

## Openers
- Tell me about yourself. (Coach: 60 seconds; past, present, why this job.)
- Why do you want to work in customer support?
- Why this company?
- What do you know about our product or customers?

## Behavioral (answer with a STAR story)
- Tell me about a time you dealt with an upset customer.
- Tell me about a time you didn't know the answer. What did you do?
- Describe a time you had to learn a new system or tool quickly.
- Tell me about a time you made a mistake with a customer and how you fixed it.
- Tell me about a time you went above and beyond.
- Describe a time you handled several things at once.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker or a policy.
- Tell me about feedback you got and what you changed.

## Scenarios
- A customer wants a refund our policy doesn't allow. What do you say?
- You have three chats open and a phone call comes in. How do you handle it?
- A customer is yelling and using rude language. What do you do?
- (Help desk) A user says "my computer is slow" / "I can't log in" / "the printer doesn't work." Walk me through what you'd check.
- (Help desk) How do you explain a technical fix to someone who isn't technical?
- When would you escalate a ticket?

## Remote work
- Describe your home work setup.
- How do you stay focused and on schedule working from home?
- How do you communicate with a team you don't see in person?
- Are you available for evenings, weekends or holidays?

## Practical
- What's your typing speed?
- What pay range are you looking for? (Coach: use the posting's range if it lists one, or his own Career Profile pay; never state market pay unless you looked it up and say where. It's fine to ask their range first.)
- When can you start?

## His questions for them (have 2 ready)
- What does a great first 90 days look like in this role?
- How is success measured (for example CSAT, handle time, tickets resolved)?
- What training do new team members get?
- What do you enjoy about working here?
