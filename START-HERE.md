# Start here

After the skills are installed (see INSTALL.md), copy the message below, paste it into Claude Duet, and send it. That's it. Claude takes it from there, one question at a time.

---

Hi Claude! I'm looking for a job in customer service, remote support, or entry-level help desk.

I'm new to AI, so please keep it simple: short messages, plain words, and one question at a time.

Please start my job onboarding interview so you can learn about me. After that, help me build my resume.

If I get stuck I'll type **help**. I'll type **skip** to skip a question, and **pause** to stop and come back later.

---

## Things you can say any time
- **help**: explain again, more simply
- **skip**: skip this question
- **pause**: save my progress and stop
- **continue my onboarding**: pick up where we left off
- **build my resume**
- **do my LinkedIn**
- **here's a job** (then paste the job posting or link)
- **is this a scam?** (then paste the message or posting)
- **practice an interview**
- **what's next?**

## Good to know
- Claude writes; you click. You submit every application, update LinkedIn, and send every email yourself.
- Claude will never ask for your Social Security number, bank details or passwords. If anything does, stop.
- Your resume and notes stay in your own Google Drive or on your computer.
