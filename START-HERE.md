# Start here

Nothing to download or install. Copy the whole message below, paste it as your very first message to Claude Duet, and send it. Claude reads the kit from the links in the message and takes it from there, one question at a time.

---

Claude writes and I click: I submit every application, update LinkedIn, and send every email myself.

If I get stuck I'll type **help**. I'll type **skip** to skip a question, and **pause** to stop and come back later.

---

## Things you can say any time
- **help**: explain again, more simply
- **skip**: skip this question
- **pause**: save my progress and stop
- **continue my onboarding**: pick up where we left off
- **start over**: restart the current step (your Career Profile stays)
- **build my resume**
- **do my LinkedIn**
- **here's a job** (then paste the job posting or link)
- **is this a scam?** (then paste the message or posting)
- **practice an interview**
- **what's next?**

## Good to know
- Claude writes; you click. You submit every application, update LinkedIn, and send every email yourself.
- Claude will never ask for your Social Security number, bank details or passwords. If anything does, stop.
- Your resume and notes stay in your own Google Drive.
