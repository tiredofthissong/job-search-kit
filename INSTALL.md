# Install (about 15 minutes, on a computer)

Do this once, on a computer browser, ideally with a friend beside you. Uploading skills from the phone app isn't confirmed to work.

What you need: a Claude account on a plan that includes Claude Duet (Pro or higher, US), signed in at claude.ai.

## 1. Download the skills
1. Open this kit's page on GitHub and click the **dist** folder.
2. Click each `.zip` file, then click the **Download** button (the arrow icon, top right of the file view). There are 7:
   `job-onboarding.zip`, `resume-builder.zip`, `linkedin-writer.zip`, `job-scam-check.zip`, `job-tailor.zip`, `interview-coach.zip`, `job-tracking.zip`.
3. Don't open or unzip them. Leave them in your Downloads folder.

## 2. Turn on file creation
1. Go to claude.ai and click your name or initials (bottom left), then **Settings**.
2. Open **Capabilities**.
3. Turn on **Create and edit files** (it may be called "Code execution and file creation"). It's usually on already.

## 3. Upload the skills
1. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**.
2. Click **+**, then **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**.
3. Pick `job-onboarding.zip` from Downloads.
4. Repeat for the other 6 zip files.
5. Check that all 7 show in the Skills list and are switched on.

If an upload fails, try that one again. If it still fails, ask your friend who shared this kit.

## 4. Connect Google (recommended)
1. In claude.ai, open **Connectors** (under **Customize** or **Settings**, depending on the version).
2. Connect **Google Drive** (Claude saves your resume and tracker there) and **Gmail** (Claude can save follow-up emails as drafts for you to send).
3. Optional: **Indeed** or **ZipRecruiter** connectors help search for jobs. You still apply on their sites yourself.

## 5. Start
Open Claude Duet, copy the message in START-HERE.md, paste it, and send.

## First-day check
- After the resume is made, make sure you can open or download `Resume - Master.docx`. If you can't find it, ask Claude: "Where did you save my resume?"

## When the kit is updated
Your friend will tell you. Download the changed zip from **dist**, then in **Customize > Skills**, remove the old version of that skill and upload the new one.

Note: menu names on claude.ai change from time to time. If something here doesn't match, look for the closest name, or ask Claude in a normal chat: "Where do I upload a skill?"
